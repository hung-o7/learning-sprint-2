The invisible extra byte

A C-style string ends with a zero byte. An 8-byte buffer holds at most 7 characters plus NUL. Try “Off by one” and step through the ninth write.

Data can cross a boundary

Unchecked copying may corrupt nearby data, including a saved frame pointer or return address. A corrupted return address can cause a crash or change control flow.

Check the limit before writing

Turn on the bounded copy and repeat. This model copies at most capacity − 1 characters, then writes NUL. Extra input is truncated; software should handle that explicitly.

Check your understanding

How many characters fit in an 8-byte buffer? Why does copying exactly 8 characters still overflow?

Reveal the answer

Seven characters fit. Eight characters fill all eight bytes, so the trailing NUL becomes a ninth write and lands outside the buffer.
